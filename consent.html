Welcome to SensoFood

This platform explores individual sensory responses related to food.

The assessment is designed to help researchers understand differences in food-sensory experiences.

Before You Begin

  • You may stop the assessment at any time.
  • Your responses will be used for research purposes.
  • Please answer the questions according to your own sensory experience.
