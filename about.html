About SensoFood

Digital Food-Sensory Research Platform

Understanding Sensory Responses

SensoFood is a digital research prototype designed to explore individual sensory responses related to food.

The platform focuses on the collection, organization and exploration of food-sensory information through a structured digital environment.

Sensory responses may vary between individuals. Understanding these differences can provide valuable information for sensory research and personalized sensory profiling.

SensoFood is designed as a research-oriented platform that can support future statistical analysis, sensory fingerprinting and data-driven research applications.
